Reading the Table

The table below illustrates how selected technology occupations map onto the proposed taxonomy. Each row synthesizes coded task patterns, role families, and skill emphases derived from qualitative analysis of O*NET descriptions. The goal is to demonstrate how multiple facets combine to form coherent career profiles.
