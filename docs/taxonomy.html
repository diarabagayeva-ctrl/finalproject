Faceted Structure

The taxonomy organizes technology occupations into a set of consistent facets that can be recombined to describe diverse roles. Each facet is coded from O*NET descriptors and aligned with language gathered from stakeholder interviews. The structure is intentionally neutral so that it can be adapted to curricula, advising materials, or platform navigation without privileging any single employer vocabulary.

Facets operate together rather than as a linear hierarchy. Users can read a career profile through one facet at a time or compare intersections, such as how different role families emphasize particular task patterns or skill sets. This approach supports transparent, multi-dimensional exploration.

Facets and Descriptions

Knowledge

Conceptual foundations and disciplinary domains that inform reasoning, such as computing principles, data ethics, business processes, or human-centered design frameworks.

Skills

Applied capabilities that can be observed in practice, including programming, data querying, requirements elicitation, documentation, coordination, and iterative improvement.

Personality

Workstyle dispositions that shape how individuals engage with tasks and teams, such as analytical curiosity, adaptability, dependability, and attention to detail.

Task Patterns

Recurring types of activities and outputs, including building software artifacts, analyzing datasets, orchestrating projects, or maintaining systems over time.

Role Families

Higher-level clusters that group occupations with similar purposes, such as software development, data and analytics, systems and infrastructure, or product and project leadership.

Using the Taxonomy

The taxonomy is designed to be read at multiple levels of granularity. Educators can use the facets to align learning outcomes with industry expectations. Career services staff can highlight how a learner's experiences map to role families. Students and career changers can compare task patterns to identify adjacent roles that fit their preferences. Because each facet is articulated with concise descriptors, the structure remains accessible while still grounded in research data.
