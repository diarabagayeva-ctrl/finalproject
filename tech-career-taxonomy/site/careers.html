How to Read the Profiles

Each profile illustrates how a career can be described using the taxonomy. Knowledge, skills, personality, and task orientation are presented as concise statements derived from the coded data. The goal is to show how multiple facets combine to create a holistic picture of practice.

Computer Programmer

Knowledge: Solid grounding in algorithms, data structures, and software architecture patterns; awareness of computing ethics and version control concepts.

Skills: Writes and maintains code, debugs issues, documents technical decisions, and collaborates with peers through code reviews and iterative releases.

Personality: Prefers systematic problem solving, attends closely to detail, and persists through complex troubleshooting tasks.

Task / Role Orientation: Builds and refines software components that meet specified requirements, translating user stories into reliable code artifacts.

Information Technology Project Manager

Knowledge: Familiar with project management methodologies, budgeting, risk controls, and the business domains of sponsored initiatives.

Skills: Plans work breakdowns, sequences dependencies, facilitates communication among technical and non-technical stakeholders, and adjusts schedules as constraints shift.

Personality: Demonstrates dependability, calm under pressure, and a collaborative mindset that keeps dispersed teams aligned.

Task / Role Orientation: Orchestrates project lifecycles, balancing scope, resources, and quality to deliver technology solutions that fit organizational needs.

Data Analyst

Knowledge: Understands statistics, database concepts, and subject-matter context needed to frame questions and interpret findings responsibly.

Skills: Extracts and cleans data, writes queries, builds visualizations, and communicates insights through reports and stakeholder briefings.

Personality: Curious and evidence-focused, comfortable with ambiguity, and attentive to the implications of data quality and bias.

Task / Role Orientation: Investigates patterns in data to inform decisions, connecting analytical outputs to practical recommendations for teams or clients.
